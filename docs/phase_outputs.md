# TBIE Phase Outputs Documentation

This document outlines the files generated by each phase of the Temporal Behavioural Intelligence Engine (TBIE) pipeline during offline training and execution.

## Phase 1 — Raw Data Validation (`src/01_validate_raw.py`)
Validates raw data, identifies ghost cohorts, and establishes clipping bounds.
*Outputs to `validation/`:*
- `raw_data_audit.md`
- `ghost_cohort.csv`
- `ghost_cohort_summary.json`
- `clip_bounds.json`
- `tier_history_schema.md`
- `date_parse_check.csv`
- `duplicate_check.csv`

## Phase 2 — Build Spine (`src/02_build_spine.py`)
Establishes the static member spine and PLCC cardholder flags.
*Outputs to `spine/`:*
- `member_spine.parquet`
- `cardholder_flags.parquet`
*Outputs to `validation/`:*
- `phase2_summary.txt`

## Phase 3 — Snapshot Builder (`src/03_snapshot_builder.py`)
Aggregates member events and transactions up to 12 monthly observation dates.
*Outputs to `snapshots/`:*
- `snapshot_YYYY_MM_DD.parquet` (x12 months)
*Outputs to `validation/`:*
- `phase3_summary.txt`

## Phase 4 — Feature Engine (`src/04_feature_engine.py`)
Engineers all 9 behavioural feature families across the 12 snapshots.
*Outputs to `features/`:*
- `features_YYYY_MM_DD.parquet` (x12 months)

## Phase 5 — Feature Validator (`src/05_feature_validator.py`)
Offline validation of feature bounds, missingness, and cross-snapshot stability.
*Outputs to `validation/`:*
- `feature_validation_report_YYYY_MM.md` (x12 months)
- `cross_snapshot_stability.json`
- `feature_validation_summary.md`

## Phase 6 — Segment Discovery (`src/06_segment_discovery.py`)
Fits the Layer 1 K-Means segment discovery model and assigns historical snapshots.
*Outputs to `segments/`:*
- `segment_model.pkl` (Frozen K-Means + PCA + Scaler)
- `segment_definitions.json`
- `behavioral_segments_YYYY_MM_DD.parquet` (x12 months)
*Outputs to `validation/`:*
- `segment_business_validation.csv`
- `segment_stability_report.csv`
- `segment_quality_report.md`

## Phase 7 — Lifecycle States (`src/07_lifecycle_states.py`)
Vectorized rules engine assigning Layer 2 lifecycle states.
*Outputs to `states/`:*
- `lifecycle_states_YYYY_MM_DD.parquet` (x12 months)
*Outputs to `outputs/`:*
- `state_definitions.json` (State Passport)
- `segment_state_cross_table.csv`
- `state_transition_matrix.csv`
*Outputs to `validation/`:*
- `phase7_summary.txt`

## Phase 8 — Transition Prediction (`src/08_transition_prediction.py`)
Trains the XGBoost model to predict 30-day segment transitions.
*Outputs to `models/`:*
- `segment_transition_model.pkl`
*Outputs to `docs/`:*
- `model_metrics.md`

## SHAP Analysis (`run_shap.py`)
Generates explainability metrics for the XGBoost transition model.
*Outputs to `outputs/`:*
- `shap_feature_importance.csv`
- `shap_per_class.csv`
*Outputs to `docs/`:*
- `shap_analysis.md`
