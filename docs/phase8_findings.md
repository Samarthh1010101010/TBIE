> that **selected its decision rule on the test set**. Read rows 14–18: validation
> preferred threshold calibration (0.8060 vs 0.7984), but the run reported
> `0.8138 [argmax]` — the better of the two *test* scores. Choosing argmax over
> calibration because it won on test, then quoting that score, inflates the
> headline by roughly 0.009.
